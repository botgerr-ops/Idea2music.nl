# Idea2Music Creator Hub — pilot implementation contract

Status: isolated development branch, not enabled and not deployed. No production database changes. The branch now also includes feature-flagged checkout integration and pending commission persistence. The branch includes an initial admin interface at /partner-manager.html and its Access-authenticated API. It does not yet provide creator logins, commission approval, refunds or payouts. Do not enable CREATOR_ENABLED before migration and all acceptance tests.

## Agreed commercial proposal
- Regular €15 including VAT; partner customer pays €13.50, only after introduction offer ends on 1 December 2026.
- €2 commission, €3 after 20 qualifying sales and explicit administrator promotion (prospective only; prior orders retain their snapshots).
- No discount stacking; free introductory song is not commissionable. Monthly payout from €20 net commission; manual bank transfer and documented invoice or appropriate alternative for a non-business partner.
- Proposed implementation decision: 14-day hold after recorded delivery, then explicit review of payment, refunds, chargebacks and self-referrals. This is an accounting control, not a change to consumer rights.
- Pilot partner terms and privacy wording require approval before onboarding. No automatic messages to influencers.
