Status: isolated development branch, not enabled and not deployed. No production database changes. ## Agreed commercial proposal
- Regular €15 including VAT; partner customer pays €13.50, only after introduction offer ends on 1 December 2026.
- €2 commission, €3 after 20 qualifying sales and explicit administrator promotion (prospective only; prior orders retain their snapshots).
- No discount stacking; free introductory song is not commissionable. Monthly payout from €20 net commission; manual bank transfer and documented invoice or appropriate alternative for a non-business partner.
- Proposed implementation decision: 14-day hold after recorded delivery, then explicit review of payment, refunds, chargebacks and self-referrals. This is an accounting control, not a change to consumer rights.
- Pilot partner terms and privacy wording require approval before onboarding. No automatic messages to influencers.

## Finance review increment
- CREATOR_FINANCE_ENABLED defaults off. Only an authenticated administrator can request commission review. No transfer or refund API is called. Mollie payment/refund/chargeback GET endpoints are checked anew; incomplete lists, failed calls, wrong amount, identity or mode block review. Any chargeback or non-failed/non-canceled refund blocks approval. Match the customer email to the partner email; additional fraud review remains manual. Delivery plus 14-day hold required. Rechecking an approved commission can reject it before reservation. No approved amount may be paid until payout reservation, fresh verification and post-payout corrections are implemented and tested. See https://docs.mollie.com/reference/get-payment, https://docs.mollie.com/reference/list-refunds and https://docs.mollie.com/reference/list-payment-chargebacks.

## Pilot completion checkpoint (28 November 2026)
- Public /partners.html: rate-limited same-origin application, pending status only, generic duplicate response; no automatic approval or email. Review the partner privacy notice and finalize/sign pilot agreement before onboarding. CREATOR_APPLICATION_ENABLED defaults off.
- Ambassador upgrade is an explicit admin action after at least 20 approved/reserved/paid commissions. Existing order attribution snapshots do not change. Subsequent codes inherit the upgraded tier.
- Finance: apply 0101_creator_finance.sql after 0100_creator_program.sql, on an isolated database first. CREATOR_PAYOUT_ENABLED defaults off, and also requires CREATOR_FINANCE_ENABLED and admin authentication. Reservation considers approved commissions from closed UTC months, oldest first, up to 15 items per batch; remaining items carry forward. One open batch per partner. Unallocated verified corrections are included in net commission. If the batch cannot cover corrections and meet the €20 minimum, keep it blocked for manual review. No payment, refund, email or bookkeeping API is called by the payout actions.
- Recheck every payment before reservation and again immediately before manual bank transfer. Pay only after the successful recheck and independently verified invoice/recipient bank details; record actual bank amount, bank reference and invoice/evidence reference after transfer. Never void a payout after transferring. For post-payout refunds, run a verified correction (settled refund or chargeback); one reversal per order, carried forward to a later payout. A chargeback reversal, failed/refunded partial order, negative balance on closure or excess adjustment needs manual accounting review. Automated scheduled reconciliation and accounting export are not included in this pilot. VAT/invoice treatment remains an accountant/admin decision; no automatic ledger posting.
